# Stage props — build sheet

The stage piece is designed around a **real failed bracket**. The screen is the lens; the part is
the anchor. If the part looks like a toy, the story dies.

## The part

- **Form:** a cast aluminium bracket, approximately 150 × 100 × 22 mm, with a large central boss and
  four bolt holes. The on-screen illustration (`web/public/assets/bracket-surface.svg`) is a 2D
  reference, not a manufacturing drawing.
- **Material for a stand-in:** 6061 aluminium plate, 20 mm, CNC or waterjet. Do not use a customer
  part for a public show without written permission, even if it is anonymised.
- **Root fillet:** 4 mm radius where the arm meets the boss. The crack must look like it started
  there, not in the middle of a flat face.
- **Crack:** 55–70 mm long, 0.4–0.8 mm wide, dark inside, with a light lip. Route it from the fillet
  toward the lower-right, as in the illustration. A Dremel cut followed by blackening and a thin
  oil wash reads well at 10 metres.
- **Tool mark (the decoy):** a 30 mm machining ridge 7 mm from the crack, 0.05 mm deep. It must be
  visible under the loupe but clearly surface-only.
- **Finish:** glass-bead blast, then a light oil/graphite wash. Matte, not shiny. A painted part
  reads as a prop; a grimy part reads as hardware.
- **Label:** `BRK-0417-B` laser-etched or stamped on a non-cracked face.

## Print / paint notes

- If the part is printed, use a resin or PLA print at 0.12 mm and fill the layers; sand the outer
  faces. Print the crack as a separate inset and glue it in, then blacken it. A printed crack that
  is part of the mesh always looks like a groove.
- Avoid high-gloss paint. Use matte clear coat.
- The `APPROVED` stamp is a separate rubber stamp, not a printed overlay.

## Stand and camera

- A stable stand or a tripod with a phone clamp. The screen does the optical work; the physical
  loupe on a lanyard is used once so the audience sees the metaphor.
- Light from one side, low and warm, plus a red key for the failure claim. Amber for the reveal;
  white for the verdict.
- Keep the part on a black bench mat. The red/amber/white key light cue is in the beat sheet.

## Printed case sheet

Use the report page (`/#/reports`) → Print / PDF. Print on warm white, 120 gsm, one page. The
`APPROVED` stamp lands on the verdict beat; the sound of the stamp is the cue.

## Safety and rights

- Deburr all edges; the prop is handled in front of an audience.
- No customer part, customer image or MVTec AD image in the public demo. See
  `docs/data-provenance.md`.
- The crack is a prop. Do not imply that a real failure investigation was done on the stand-in
  without labelling it.
